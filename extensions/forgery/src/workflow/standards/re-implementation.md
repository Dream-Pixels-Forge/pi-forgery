# Standard: Re-Implementation

Adapted from Google's Elephant-Goldfish Model (Apache-2.0). Stage 2 (Comprehension).

## Purpose
Evaluate whether a stranger could re-implement the design exactly, without
asking the author questions.

## Review vectors
- **Defined terms**: Is every acronym, initialism, and domain term defined on
  first use?
- **No missing context**: Are "the system", "the service", "it", "the config"
  always specific?
- **No hand-waving**: Are phrases like "it should just work", "details TBD",
  "handle errors appropriately" absent?
- **Self-contained**: Is all needed knowledge in the document or its reading
  list?
- **Logical flow**: Are steps in order with no jumps or assumptions?

## Outcome
Findings only. Blockers: critical undefined terms or missing context that make
re-implementation impossible.
